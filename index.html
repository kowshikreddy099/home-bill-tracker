Your bill history

One row per month, oldest first, no gaps. First column is month as YYYY-MM, then one column per bill in rupees. Paste your own table here, or the contents of ml/bills.csv. You need 16 or more months for the ML models.

The models are Repeat last month, 3-month average, Ridge regression and Boosted trees. Each is tested by training on earlier months and predicting the next one, and the model with the smallest average error predicts next month. Data stays in this browser.
